hecho+

One email a day with three things to do in Spanish. Each one opens in the right app, and everything you do goes into one record.

Each email

  • A tenseThe next level in verbos, or a review of the verbs you keep getting wrong.
  • A conversationA theme in charla, chosen from your interests and what you talked about before.
  • An articleA real article at your level on something you care about, read with traduce.

Record

Every tense, conversation and article you finish is kept in one place, with the Spanish words you looked up along the way. It shows the work you have done, with no streaks or scores.

Early access

hecho+ is not built yet. Leave your email and we will write once, when it is ready.

We only use your email to tell you about hecho+. See privacy.
