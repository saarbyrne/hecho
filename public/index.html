Hecho

Three free tools for practising Spanish. They run in your browser, with no account and no points.

Apps

Verbos

Verb conjugation in 21 levels. Each level has a short lesson and a typed quiz, and a daily review brings back what you got wrong.

Web app · works offline

Open Verbos

Charla

Talk out loud in Spanish with an AI partner that remembers your earlier conversations and picks a new theme each day.

Web app · needs a free Gemini key

Open Charla

Traduce

Select Spanish on any web page and read the English in the corner of the screen. It also works from English to Spanish.

Extension · Chrome, Edge, Brave, Dia

Add to browser

Privacy

Your practice stays in your own browser. There is no account, and nothing is stored on a server.

Hecho+

A paid version is in the works. It links the three apps with one daily email and one history of everything you have done.
