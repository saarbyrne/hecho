    How it works

    1

    Select.

    Highlight Spanish or English text on any page.

    2

    Translate.

    Click the button beside your selection, or press the shortcut. On Windows it is Alt+T.

    ⌥+T

    3

    Read or replace.

    The translation appears in a card in the corner. In a text field, Reemplazar swaps your text for it.

    Set up

    Ready in a minute.

    1. Open traduce in the Chrome Web Store.
    2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
    3. Select some text on any page and translate it.

    Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and other Chromium browsers. The traduce button in your toolbar opens a box for typing text and lets you change the shortcut.

    Questions

    Is it free?
    Yes. There is no paid version. MyMemory allows about 5,000 characters a day when the built-in model is not available.
    Which browsers does it work in?
    Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and other Chromium browsers, version 116 or later.
    Can I change the shortcut?
    Yes. Open the traduce button in your toolbar and click the shortcut, or go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
