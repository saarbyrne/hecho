Privacy

Apps. Verbos, Charla and Traduce keep your practice in your own browser. Hecho has no accounts and no server that stores it. Charla sends your conversation to Google Gemini with your own key. Traduce sends selected text to MyMemory when your browser cannot translate it on the device.

Hecho+ sign-up. If you leave your email, we store it with the date you signed up. We use it only to write about Hecho+. We do not share or sell it. To have it deleted, write to hola@hecho.fyi.

Support. Payments are handled by Stripe under its own privacy policy. We see your name, email and the amount, and never your card details.

This site. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
